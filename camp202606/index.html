Prep Notes
Key Tips
⛺ Quest Zion Tent Setup
  • Attach rainfly before nightfall every night — Vermont weather shifts fast
  • Ground cloth under tent to stop moisture wicking up
  • Stake all guy-lines — large tent catches wind
  • Keep mesh vents open even with rainfly (prevents condensation)
  • Bring a mallet — VT ground can be rocky
👧 Traveling with a 4-Year-Old
  • Kids' life jacket for paddling (bring your own for fit)
  • Waterproof jacket + boots — trails get muddy after rain
  • Portable potty for nighttime without walking to bathrooms
  • Kid headlamp — they'll love having their own
  • Small comfort toy or stuffed animal from home
  • Children's Benadryl + antihistamine cream for bug bites
🦟 Bugs & Safety
  • Vermont has ticks — do a full tick check every evening
  • Black flies are common near water in early June — cover up
  • Bug spray with DEET or Picaridin (Picaridin safer for kids)
  • ⚠️ Active railroad runs near the lake trail — stay on marked paths
  • Teach your child the safety whistle rule if separated
🚗 Hyundai Tucson Hybrid
  • ~38 mpg highway = ~5 gal round trip, minimal stops
  • Load cargo carefully — rear visibility matters on VT back roads
  • Download offline Google Maps before leaving; spotty coverage in Dorset
  • Park phone: (802) 362-1655 — call ahead to confirm site
🏊 Dorset Quarry Notes
  • Parking fee applies — bring cash or card
  • Shallow marble ledges safe for toddlers; avoid deep jump areas
  • No lifeguard — supervise closely with a 4-year-old
  • Bring shade umbrella + chairs — very little natural shade
⛽ Cooking Tips
  • Pre-chop and pre-marinate at home — saves time & dishes
  • Bring extra fuel canister for the camp stove
  • Bear box / hang food bag at night — VT has black bears
  • Biodegradable camp soap for washing up
  • Paper plates for easy cleanup on busy days
